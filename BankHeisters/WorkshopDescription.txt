[h1]白子与星野（泳装）领导的阿拜多斯：先睹为快[/h1]

戴好面罩，准备武器，到点了整点钱花！本文明包中包含了两位来自《蔚蓝档案》游戏中的角色，两位来自阿拜多斯高中的同学一起抢过银行，现在还要共同在《文明》中出场。

[b]阿拜多斯[/b]曾是一座繁荣的学校，随着气候变化却逐渐衰亡。如今，它在最后的学生的领导下，正在努力恢复往日的荣光。作为[b]绿洲学园[/b]，阿拜多斯从淡水沙漠和绿洲单元格中获得额外的生产力、食物和金币。在研发完成自然历史市政时，他们还将从国境内的绿洲中立即获得金币和文化值。他们的特色区域是[b]阿拜多斯分校[/b]，替代学院区域。阿拜多斯分校在一座城市中可以多次建设，且造价更加便宜。在城市内建造多座分校便可使它们获得与区域相邻的相邻加成，而在绿洲和沙漠泛滥平原旁它们的产出还能进一步提升。

阿拜多斯的特色单位是[b]火力支援无人机[/b]。这种替代火箭炮的单位更早解锁，并且获得了无人机带来的额外战斗力、射程和视野范围。不过，它不会再受到无人机的加成。

身为对策委员会的行动队长，[b]白子[/b]的领袖特性是[b]银行差事[/b]。他们的单位善于劫掠，不仅在掠夺时花费更少的行动力，更可以从敌方的国库中取得金钱！如果掠夺区域能够获得金币，他们还将同时获得文化。

与之不同的是，[b]星野（泳装）[/b]更喜欢轻松地赢得文化胜利。她的领袖特性[b]度假区复兴[/b]使得绿洲和沙漠泛滥平原为相邻单元格额外+2魅力，并可以解锁特色改良设施[b]阿拜多斯度假区[/b]。这种建设在海滨的改良设施能够提供额外的文化，并在研究飞行科技之后成为文化胜利的动力之一。

身为联邦搜查社团“沙勒”（也译“夏莱”）的老师，您将如何带领阿拜多斯走上复兴之路？您将会如何重建一个文明，令其经得起时间的考验？

[h1]我们的风格：[/h1]

[b]有玩法，不超模。[/b]每位领袖的能力组都经过精心设计与测试，使它们能够支持一种独特的玩法。能力强度与官方的强力领袖相当而不会击碎原作平衡。

[b]忠于原作。[/b]我们的作品在制作过程中精细考证了领袖和他们的故事，拥有完整的人名、地名、城市名称和外交用语，以尽最大可能表现原作的风土人情和领袖性格特征。

[b]广泛兼容。[/b]所有已制作的美术素材均参照《文明6》游戏的规格进行处理，确保其在各种分辨率和设置下都能正确显示且整洁清爽。

[b]领袖语音。[/b]使用了原作游戏中的语音片段。不含AI生成内容。

[b]负责任的AI使用。[/b]我们谨慎地使用生成式AI创作的内容，确保不含“数字泔水”。

[h1]Shiroko and Hoshino (Swimsuit) lead Abydos: First Look[/h1]

Wear your masks and gear up with your weapons, it's time to rob some gold! This civilization pack contains 2 characters from Blue Archive. The two students of Abydos High School robbed a bank together, and is coming to the Civ game together.

[b]Abydos[/b] was once a school with prosperity but declined due to climate change. Now under the leadership of its last students, it is struggling to rise up again. As an [b]Oasitic Campus[/b], Abydos gain additional Production, Food and Gold from Freshwater Desert and Oasis tiles. They also receive Gold and Culture from each Oasis tile in their territory when they unlock Natural History civic. Their unique district is [b]Abydos Branch School[/b] which replaces Campus. The Abydos Branch School can be built multiple times in a city, and is cheaper. Build multiple Abydos Branch School and they can gain adjacency bonuses from adjacent districts, while they will be more productive if they are placed near Oasis or Desert Floodplains tiles.

The unique unit of Abydos is [b]Fire Support Drone[/b] which replaces Rocket Artillery and unlocks earlier. Is has enhanced Range and Sight and is stronger, but won't receive bonuses from Drones.

[b]Shiroko[/b], the field captain of Countermeasure Committee, has [b]Bank Job[/b] leader ability. Their units are adept at pillaging, cost less Movement to pillage, and can even seize Gold from enemy treasury! If pillaging a district can reward them with Gold, they can receive Culture at the same time.

Different from Shiroko, [b]Hoshino (Swimsuit)[/b] prefer to win a Culture Victory effortlessly. Her leader ability [b]Resort Restoration[/b] grants additional Appeal to Oasis and Desert Floodplains, and unlocks unique improvement [b]Abydos Resort[/b], which must be built in coastal tiles, provide additional Culture, and can become a boost to Culture Victory with Flight technology.

As the Sensei of Independent Federal Investigation Club S.C.H.A.L.E., how will you lead Abydos to a restoration? How will you rebuild a civilization, and let it stand the test of time?

[h1]Our Way of Modding:[/h1]

[b]Yes for Uniqueness, No for Overpowering[/b]: All leaders are designed and tested so that each of their ability sets promotes a unique play-style. They are set to be as powerful as strong official leaders and won't break game balancing.

[b]Lore-Loyalty[/b]: Our mods are made with a fine research of the leaders and their stories, and provides full lists of named places, citizen names, city names and diplomacy statements to better represent lore and leader characteristics.

[b]Optimized for Compatibility[/b]: All art assets included in this mod are processed to fit the need of Civilization VI, so that they can display correctly and neatly on different screens / settings.

[b]Leader Speeches[/b]: Voice clips from the original game are used. Not AI-generated.

[b]Responsible AI Usage[/b]: We use generative-AI-created contents with caution to ensure the mod is slop-free.
